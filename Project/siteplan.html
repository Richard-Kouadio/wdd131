StudyHub

Site Name

StudyHub

StudyHub was chosen because it represents a central place where students can access learning resources, study materials, and academic support.

Optional Domain: studyhub.org

Site Purpose

StudyHub provides educational resources, study guides, learning tips, and downloadable notes that help students improve their academic performance.

Scenarios

  • Where can I download study materials for my courses?
  • What are the best study tips for preparing for exams?

Color Schema

  • Dark Blue (#1E3A8A) - Used for headers, navigation, headings, buttons, and footer.
  • Light Gray (#F3F4F6) - Used for page background and content sections.

Typography

  • Poppins - Used for headings.
  • Roboto - Used for paragraphs and lists.
