About Côte d'Ivoire

Côte d'Ivoire is a country located in West Africa. It is known for its cultural diversity, tropical climate, beautiful landscapes, and agricultural production.

Capital: Yamoussoukro

Largest City: Abidjan

Population: Approximately 30 million people

Official Language: French

Currency: West African CFA franc
